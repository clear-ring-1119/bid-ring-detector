How the score works

  • overlap: shared tenders divided by the tenders of the less active firm.
  • behaviour: share of shared tenders won by the pair's recurring core group.
  • timing: share of shared tenders where both bids came in within 10 minutes.
  • registry: 1.0 shared director, 0.75 shared address only.

Flag threshold: 0.5. Settings are in config_used.json.
